# Opt-in native identity automation

`ghimera.identity-automation/1` connects Collector to bounded source-local
proposals, a separate model review, the existing reversible graph history, and
`ghimera.graph-planning/5`. This is not model-accuracy evidence or full I07
acceptance. Controlled test wires witness contracts and failures only.

The [safe fragment](../examples/identity-automation.toml) must be integrated into
an operator's complete validated recipe. It supplies no endpoint, model pin,
sink path, resource allocation or credential. Required inputs are semantic
roles, proposer/reviewer bindings, independence policy, mention/pair/call/context/
evidence/reason limits, and an explicit temporal planning date. Native graph
resolution, a journal and retained `model_work` results are required. Identity
calls charge the original judge budget; no limit is extended. The effective
non-secret configuration remains in the ordinary receipt/journal.

## Connected native route

After collection/local intake and before an ordinary next research plan,
`GoalLoop.resolve_identity` runs `IdentityStage`. Same-role candidates retain
original mention IDs, including different scripts/surfaces. Labels alone never
resolve identity. Selection keeps whole exact admitted native windows and
reports omitted mentions/pairs; it invents no canonical-name registry.

`SelfHostedModel.identity_propose` and `identity_review` use isolated versioned
contracts/prompts. Each has its own native `ModelInvocation` intent, charged
reservation, original acknowledged output bytes and actual call evidence.
Review binds the proposal digest and independently checks identity, role and
temporal support. Missing, ambiguous or unsupported checks stay unresolved.
Dates require their exact ISO representation in offered native evidence; other
formats are not repaired. `require_distinct_models = true` refuses identical
model identities or identical served routes. Setting it false explicitly records
shared-model review; it never collapses the two invocations into one.

Under the existing graph writer lock, `ResearchGraph.append` admits a
`ghimera.identity-decision/2` only from exact prior native proposal/review ACKs
and unchanged graph checkpoint/history. Call-shaped caller telemetry alone is
refused. The basis is `model_reviewed`, never `human_reviewed`. Actual calls,
population/proposal/review digests and source evidence remain in the SAME graph
history; original source-local nodes/edges are unchanged. Conflicts are refused,
not silently resolved by retracting prior decisions.

The existing manual `decide_identity(operation="retract", ...)` reverses model
decisions with unchanged manual provenance. A native journal `identity_history`
observation projects acknowledged manual additions into the next plan; it cannot
adopt unacknowledged model effects. Planning/5 includes reversible history and
the resolved view at `resolved_as_of`, preserving original endpoints/references.
Dated merges are not generalized when no date is selected. The whole history/
member closure is selected or omitted with a count, never partially clipped.
Prefix replay keeps earlier plans unchanged by later decisions.

## Failure boundaries and open acceptance

Original reservations survive crashes/reopen. After a durable model ACK but
lost application, exact stored bytes are reusable without another contact or
spend reset. UNKNOWN identity outcomes remain held even if another policy allows
more unanswered calls; there is no retry switch. Graph ACK followed by lost
journal observation also remains held. Arbitrary graph/source/model-effect
adoption and caller reconciliation of such effects are outside this increment.

Each inspected immutable pair is recorded once. Ambiguous pairs require new
source-local evidence for reconsideration. Later bounded passes may inspect
truncated pairs only within remaining original quotas. The stage invents no
aliases, rewrites no text, infers no office-holder relationships, fabricates no
human approval and does not replace semantic extraction/review. Real model and
whole-Collector quality acceptance for homonyms, aliases and dated offices remain
open. Disabled recipes/manual decisions/older planning identities are unchanged;
legacy semantic client schemas and prompt fingerprints remain frozen. Identity
calls have their own full operational service schema.
